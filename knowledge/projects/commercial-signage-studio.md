## Overview
A modern, editorial web showcase for a premier commercial signage and architectural brand fabrication studio, featuring interactive project galleries, 3D signage material specs, client showcases, and custom quotation forms.

## Problem & Challenge
Signage fabrication clients (corporate offices, retail stores, healthcare facilities) require granular visual proof of material finishes, illumination techniques, and engineering tolerances before placing high-value orders.

## Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Modern Next.js Architecture**: Built with Next.js 16 (React 19) utilizing server components for rapid initial paint and zero unnecessary client JavaScript bundles.
- **Fluid Visual Animations**: Crafted smooth portfolio transitions, material comparison sliders, and interactive dimension previews using Motion.
- **Lead Capture & Inquiry Routing**: Implemented structured RFQ (Request for Quotation) workflows allowing prospective clients to specify dimensions, lighting styles, and architectural substrates.

## Engineering Outcomes
- Premium aesthetic aligning with high-end commercial clients and architects.
- 98+ Google Lighthouse performance score with sub-second LCP (Largest Contentful Paint).
