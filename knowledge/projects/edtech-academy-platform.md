## Overview
A high-engagement digital learning and exam preparation web platform for students seeking admissions and competitive coaching, featuring interactive course syllabi, faculty profiles, mock exam schedules, and registration workflows.

## Problem & Challenge
Educational coaching academies need to convey academic rigor and course credibility through an engaging, intuitive digital presence that converts visiting students and parents into enrolled applicants.

## Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Interactive Component Design**: Developed fluid micro-interactions, course module accordions, and celebratory achievement animations using Framer Motion and Canvas Confetti.
- **Client-Side Routing**: Implemented seamless navigation across subjects, exam preparation tracks, and fee structures using React Router.
- **Mobile-Responsive Accessibility**: Designed accessible touch-friendly navigation ensuring full usability across budget Android smartphones and tablets.

## Engineering Outcomes
- Increased course inquiry conversions through clear curriculum visualization.
- High-performing, lightweight single-page application loading in under 1 second.
