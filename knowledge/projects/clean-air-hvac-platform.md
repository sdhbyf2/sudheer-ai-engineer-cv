## Overview
A technical engineering web platform for an industrial HVAC and cleanroom air handling specialist, featuring HVAC equipment specifications, airflow capacity calculators, and validated RFQ lead funnels.

## Problem & Challenge
Industrial cleanroom clients (pharmaceutical labs, microelectronics manufacturers, hospital operating rooms) require exacting air change per hour (ACH) calculations and compliance assurance before commissioning installations.

## Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Strict Client-Side Validation**: Engineered multi-step engineering quote request forms validated with Zod schemas and React Hook Form, capturing room dimensions, particulate standards (ISO 14644), and filtration grades (HEPA/ULPA).
- **Interactive Mechanical Showcase**: Built animated HVAC unit diagrams and airflow cycle visualizations using Framer Motion.
- **Server-Side API Handling**: Processed and routed technical inquiries with validated JSON payloads and email dispatchers.

## Engineering Outcomes
- Reduced back-and-forth qualification calls by capturing complete mechanical specifications on initial submission.
- High-performance, responsive layout optimized for facility managers and technical buyers.
