## Overview
A customer-facing telecom and internet service provider (ISP) portal enabling subscribers to explore fiber-optic internet plans, test bandwidth speeds, manage account subscriptions, and submit support tickets.

## Problem & Challenge
ISP customers require clear, real-time comparisons between bandwidth tiers (download/upload speeds, data limits, pricing) and instant self-service account management without calling customer support.

## Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Plan Comparison Engine**: Built an interactive broadband plan selector with dynamic pricing calculators and speed tier filtering.
- **Responsive Telecom UI**: Structured responsive layouts optimized for fast loading on mobile and desktop devices.
- **RESTful API Services**: Connected backend service endpoints for billing inquiries, coverage checks, and customer service requests.

## Engineering Outcomes
- Simplified broadband plan selection and customer onboarding.
