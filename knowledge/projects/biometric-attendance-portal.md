## Overview
An enterprise web application managing candidate registrations, biometric device attendance synchronization, administrative reporting, and automated payment gateway processing.

## Problem & Challenge
High-volume applicant registration workflows require continuous synchronization with physical biometric capture devices and secure real-time payment reconciliation without duplicate transaction records.

## Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Biometric Integration & Cron Sync**: Connected biometric hardware endpoints with automated background cron schedulers to ingest and verify attendance logs continuously.
- **Payment Gateway Reconciliation**: Integrated the Atompay merchant payment gateway with cryptographic checksum validation and instant transaction status webhooks.
- **Administrative Reporting Dashboard**: Developed granular role-based administrative portals for applicant search, fee audit trails, and biometric compliance verification.

## Engineering Outcomes
- Automated attendance tracking and eliminated manual spreadsheet reconciliation.
- Processed high-volume application batches with verified payment settlement.
