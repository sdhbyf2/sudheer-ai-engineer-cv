## Overview
Delivered a digital legal services platform, internal case management system, and staff clocking application for a professional UK legal services organisation and barrister chambers (3 Bolt Court).

## Problem & Challenge
Legal chambers require a resilient, service-oriented digital platform capable of handling confidential case workflows, advocate scheduling, billable hours tracking, and secure payment processing with fast response times.

## Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Service-Oriented Microservices & Caching**: Developed application functionality using a service-oriented microservices architecture and Redis caching for fast data access.
- **Payment & Backend Integrations**: Integrated payment-related functionality, automated PDF billing exports (jsPDF), DocuSign eSign workflows, and supporting backend components.
- **Infrastructure & Deployment**: Supported cloud infrastructure provisioning and application deployment using Terraform.

## Engineering Outcomes
- Streamlined legal case management, staff clocking, and payment workflows for chambers staff and clients.
- Reliable, scalable deployment managed via Terraform and Redis caching.
