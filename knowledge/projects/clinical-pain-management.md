## Overview
A patient-focused web platform for a specialized clinical pain management and physical rehabilitation clinic, presenting therapeutic methodologies, clinician credentials, treatment pathways, and appointment inquiries.

## Problem & Challenge
Chronic pain patients require empathetic, easy-to-read clinical explanations without technical medical jargon, paired with immediate clarity on clinic locations, accessibility, and booking procedures.

## Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Patient-Centric Information Architecture**: Organized complex therapy categories (musculoskeletal, neuropathic, rehabilitation) into digestible, high-contrast visual sections.
- **Client-Side Routing**: Implemented smooth page transitions between treatments, consultant biographies, and appointment contact forms using React Router.
- **High-Contrast Accessibility**: Designed color schemes and font hierarchies adhering to WCAG 2.1 AA standards for patients experiencing visual impairment or fatigue.

## Engineering Outcomes
- Lowered patient intake drop-offs through friction-free inquiry forms.
- Fully responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.
