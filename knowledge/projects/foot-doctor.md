## Overview
A modern digital health and clinic web platform designed for podiatry and foot specialist practices, streamlining patient discovery, clinical treatment explanations, and appointment inquiries.

## Problem & Challenge
Specialist healthcare clinics often struggle with fragmented patient communication, unintuitive treatment service navigation, and slow mobile load times on outdated CMS setups.

## Technical Architecture & Implementation
- **Frontend Architecture**: Built with modern React and TypeScript on Next.js, ensuring strong type safety and server-rendered performance.
- **Component Design System**: Structured reusable clinic components with Tailwind CSS for accessible, high-contrast healthcare presentation adhering to WCAG standards.
- **Service & Inquiries Routing**: Integrated RESTful API routes for patient booking inquiries and clinic schedule notifications.

## Engineering Outcomes
- Clean, responsive mobile-first experience with sub-second page loads.
- Fully typed component codebase with zero runtime prop errors.
