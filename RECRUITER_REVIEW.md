# Five-minute portfolio review

Site: https://sudheercv.vercel.app/

This is a review template, not collected recruiter feedback. Share it with a willing reviewer and record their observations separately.

1. Read the first screen for 10 seconds. What role does this person do, and what would you hire them to build?
2. Find one relevant project. Can you explain the problem, personal contribution and engineering decisions without guessing?
3. Open Quick CV, then return to the page. Find the downloadable PDF and an email route. Was anything difficult to discover?
4. Read a project on your phone. Note any text that feels small, clipped content or animation that distracts from reading.
5. Would you invite this person to an initial conversation? What specific evidence would strengthen your decision?

Record the browser/device, confusing wording, task completion difficulty, and one highest-priority change. Do not treat a numerical design score as hiring validation.

## Physical-device checks still needed

- iPhone Safari and Android Chrome: portrait crop, browser toolbar resizing, portrait/landscape rotation, navigation position, scrolling and return from the PDF.
- Small phone: expanded case-study readability and email link behavior in the device's mail app.
- Keyboard or assistive technology: logical reading order and focus through the menu and CV dialog.
- Reduced motion enabled in the operating system: reading and navigation remain complete.

## Evidence to add when available

- A permitted product screenshot or public demo URL for each featured system.
- The project context and exactly what was owned by Sudheer.
- Any measured outcome with its baseline, measurement method and period. Keep qualitative outcomes until these are available.

Architecture illustrations on the site explain the supplied systems; they are not product screenshots or live service demonstrations.
